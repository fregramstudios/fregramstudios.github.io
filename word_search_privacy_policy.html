Welcome to Word Search, a mobile game developed by Fregram Studios ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains our data practices for this game. By playing Word Search, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

1. Children’s Privacy & Eligibility (COPPA & GDPR)

Word Search is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA/UK). In compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children in these age groups. If we learn we have inadvertently collected data from a child under these ages, we will take immediate steps to delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at fregramstudios@gmail.com so we can work with our third-party partners to remove it.

2. Information We and Our Partners Collect

We collect information to deliver advertisements, analyze performance, provide gameplay features, and support our users. We do not process sensitive personal information. The data collected includes:

  • Information You Provide: If you contact us directly (e.g., via email for support), we collect your email address and any information you include in your message.
  • Device Identifiers: Your IP address and Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID).
  • Technical & Diagnostic Data: Device model, OS version, CPU info, battery status, memory, language preferences, and crash reports/error logs.
  • Connection & Location Data: Mobile carrier name, internet connection type (e.g., WiFi), and imprecise location data (such as country or region derived from your IP address). We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Usage Data: Gameplay progress, feature interactions, and session length via GameAnalytics.
  • Push Notifications: If you enable them, we may send notifications to your device. You can opt-out at any time via your device settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data to operate, improve, and secure our game. Specifically, we use your information to:

  • Deliver Targeted Advertising: To develop and display personalized ads tailored to your interests and general location through our advertising partners.
  • Analyze and Improve the Game: To identify usage trends, understand how players interact with Word Search, and improve overall game performance.
  • Communicate With You: To respond to your direct inquiries or support requests.
  • Protect Vital Interests & Security: To prevent fraud, ensure the security of our services, and protect the vital interests or safety of our players and the public.

Depending on your location, we rely on specific legal bases to process your information:

    • Legal Obligations & Vital Interests: To comply with applicable laws, cooperate with law enforcement, or protect the safety and rights of our players and third parties.

You have the right to request the deletion of data associated with your use of Word Search. Because we do not use account logins, we do not directly hold a profile of you.
